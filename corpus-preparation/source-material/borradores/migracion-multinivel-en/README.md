# Multilingual pilot · English renditions

These three cards are English renditions of the Spanish multi-depth pilot.
They prove that VitaMap can store a non-public content locale without exposing
an English interface or allowing English chat responses.

Status: editorial `draft` after a separate language and fidelity pass. They
are not published or indexed. The corpus administrator blocks publication
while English remains a non-public content locale. Each card shares the Spanish
`canonical_card_id`, has its own `tarjeta_id`, and records the source version
and SHA-256 checksum used for the rendition.

If a Spanish source card changes, the composition test fails until the English
rendition is reviewed and its traceability is updated.
